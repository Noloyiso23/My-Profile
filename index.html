My Manifesto

Fellow members of the Wits ProMaths Chapter, I am Noloyiso Copantsi, and I am running for Deputy Chairperson and the Academics Office. We are a society built on academic excellence and community, but to reach our full potential, we need leadership that is active in purpose. My manifesto is built on a foundation of member representation, accountability, and realistic progress.

"I won't make promises I cannot keep. I will focus on what we can control: supporting our first-years, celebrating our high achievers, and building a stronger, more connected ProMaths community."

1. Representation that Bridges the Gap
ProMaths is more than just a society; it is a support system. As your Deputy Chairperson, I want to be the bridge between the members and the committee. I don't want to just be a face you see during elections; I want to be someone you feel comfortable approaching when academic pressures rise. I will actively engage with our members to understand your daily challenges and ensure our collective voice is heard where it matters—within the committee, the faculty, and the university.

2. Accountability that Delivers Results
Too often, we hear promises that fade away after the votes are counted. I am committed to transparency. As your Deputy Chairperson, I will ensure that the initiatives we promise are tracked, executed, and communicated to you. No empty words. If we commit to improving our society's events, academic support, or social connections, I will ensure those commitments are fulfilled. You deserve a committee that answers to you, not one that just speaks for you.

3. Supporting First-Years through Mentorship
As a BSc Computer Science student, I understand the intense academic pressure we face. My goal is to ensure that we are not just surviving our courses, but thriving in them. Facilitating formal mentorship programs between senior and first-year ProMaths members is a key part of this vision. We grow stronger when we learn from each other, and I want to build a society where our senior students pour into the next generation.

4. Celebrating Excellence through Prize-Giving
ProMaths is a society that prides itself on academic excellence. To celebrate this, I will champion a formal Prize-Giving initiative for students who have performed exceptionally well in their faculty. Celebrating achievement is not just about rewards; it is about encouraging hard work, inspiring others to strive for the same, and building a culture where effort is seen, valued, and celebrated. I want to ensure that our top-performing members get the recognition they truly deserve.

I am not here to occupy a seat; I am here to serve you. Let’s build a ProMaths that we are all proud of. Let's ensure real progress, together.
